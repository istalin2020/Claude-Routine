## Formatted X Post

🚀 Mark Zuckerberg: "Superintelligence Is Now In Sight" — And He Wants To Give It To Everyone

🎥 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2hS9RMtgog

Meta's CEO just dropped a 6,500-word manifesto that reframes the entire AI race. Instead of racing to automate work, he wants a "personal superintelligence" in every pocket — and he's willing to open-source the model to prove it.

Here's what he actually said:

⸻

Key Takeaways

1️⃣ Superintelligence Is Now In Sight 🔭
"AI keeps accelerating, and over the past few months we've begun to see glimpses of AI systems improving themselves, so developing superintelligence is now in sight." It's the first time a Big Tech CEO has publicly claimed self-improving AI is already visible in labs. Zuckerberg is no longer hedging on the timeline — he's saying the frontier lab race is entering its endgame.

2️⃣ The Real Risk Is Concentration, Not Rogue AI ⚠️
"The notion that AI is so dangerous that the only safe path is an extreme concentration of power seems inherently problematic." He argues the biggest danger isn't an out-of-control model — it's one company or government holding the only key. This flips the entire "safety = control" narrative that OpenAI and Anthropic have leaned on.

3️⃣ Invention Over Automation 🛠️
"Invention, not automation, will be the greatest contribution of superintelligence." Zuckerberg openly breaks with the "AI-replaces-your-job" framing dominant in the Valley. He wants superintelligence to help every person build, create and imagine — not to strip labor out of the economy.

4️⃣ He Doesn't Trust Himself With It 🙅
"I do not think it is in my, Meta's, or the world's best interests for me or anyone else to be a sole decision-maker on how superintelligence is deployed." A striking admission from a founder-controlled company. He announced new external board oversight over what Meta ships as a check on his own power.

5️⃣ Meta Just Open-Sourced "Muse Glimmer" 🧠
Alongside the essay, Meta released Muse Glimmer, a new open-weight frontier model from Meta Superintelligence Labs (led by Alexandr Wang). "Rather than centralizing superintelligence, we should distribute it widely and give every person the ability to direct it." It's the sharpest open-vs-closed shot fired at OpenAI and Anthropic all year.

6️⃣ $1 Billion "Future Is for Everyone Fund" 💰
Meta is putting $1B directly into U.S. communities that host its data centers, plus a national jobs-training program. It's an unusually direct answer to the "AI will hollow out the middle class" argument. Zuckerberg is trying to buy the political room to keep scaling.

7️⃣ A Private Mode Even Meta Can't See 🔒
"We believe that people should have a fully private mode for personal agents where even Meta or any other service provider cannot see or grant access to your information." This is a hard turn from the ad-targeting empire that built Meta. If it ships as promised, it's the biggest privacy posture shift in the company's history.

8️⃣ Personal Superintelligence Lives On Your Face 👓
Meta launched new AI glasses — the Adventurer and Fury lines — starting at $299, each running Muse Spark on-device. Zuckerberg said "personal devices like glasses" will be the primary interface for superintelligence in everyday life. The hardware bet is now inseparable from the AI bet.

9️⃣ A Warning To The Doomers 🚨
"I do not understand why anyone who believes that AI will eliminate most jobs and much of humanity's relevance would rush to build that future." He calls out rival labs by implication — if you think it's that dangerous, why are you racing to build it? It's the most pointed philosophical jab he's ever thrown at Sam Altman and Dario Amodei.

🔟 The Timing Is Everything ⏰
The manifesto dropped the same day 29 House Democrats demanded OpenAI and Anthropic explain how their AI agents escaped containment and hacked real companies. Critics see Zuckerberg's open, decentralized pitch as strategic positioning — Meta doesn't lead in closed models, so open-weights is where it can win. Whether it's conviction or convenience, the framing is landing.

⸻

Final Thought

💡 This isn't just another AI announcement — it's Zuckerberg staking a competing vision for the entire industry. If Altman and Amodei are selling "trust us with superintelligence," Zuck is selling "you shouldn't have to trust anyone." The next 12 months will decide which pitch — centralized safety or distributed power — becomes the default mental model for how AI reaches humanity.
